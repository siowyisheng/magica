# Synthesise sound effects at runtime instead of shipping samples

Sound effects for Abilities, Resolution, damaging Hits, movement, crystals, turn phases, results and the UI are synthesised in the browser with Web Audio, from small recipes (a base timbre per school, varied per Ability), extending the synth that already plays the music. We rejected sample files (generated or sourced CC0 packs): they add repo size, licence and attribution tracking, and can't be downloaded from the build environment, whereas recipes keep the bundle small, work offline from `file://` for Electron or Tauri packaging, and can be tuned in code. Samples would sound more realistic, so an individual recipe can be replaced by a sample later without changing how sounds are triggered.

The engine stays pure: it emits structured cue data on its events (ability, role such as cast, launch, resolution, damage or absorbed) and the UI turns new events into sound. Within a Resolution the sounds are spaced in launch order, with a damage sound following each Attack that takes HP off a mage and a quieter absorbed sound when a shield or Block takes it all. Replays and spectating play the same way, and scrubbing is silent.
